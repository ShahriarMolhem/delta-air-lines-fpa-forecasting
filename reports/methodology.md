# Analysis Methodology | Undergraduate FP&A Case Study

## Purpose
Apply introductory-to-intermediate financial statement analysis and Excel-based financial planning to published Delta results, with defensible historical calculations and **illustrative** business scenarios.

## Historical reconciliation
- **Operating revenue** = passenger revenue + cargo revenue + other revenue.
- **Operating income (GAAP)** = total operating revenue − total operating expenses.
- **Operating margin** = operating income / total operating revenue.
- For FY2024→FY2025, create an *arithmetic* operating-income bridge showing changes in each revenue category and major cost category. **This is not causal attribution.**

## Operational drivers
- ASM (available seat miles) represents capacity offered; PRASM is passenger revenue per ASM, measured in cents.
- Passenger revenue can be forecast as `ASM (millions) × model passenger USD/ASM`; convert **cents to dollars** correctly.
- Fuel expense = modeled fuel consumption (million gallons) × modeled implied all-in expense per gallon (USD). Difference between year-end fuel expenses can be decomposed into price and consumption effects.
- Labor can be modeled per ASM with a stated inflation rate, and remaining operating expense can be represented by a disclosed **simplified GAAP expense residual** per ASM.
- **Other revenue** includes operations outside ticket revenue (e.g., refinery and ancillary businesses). Do not describe it as airline fares.

## FY2026–FY2028 planning scenario
Specify editable scenario assumptions for capacity, passenger yield, fuel consumption per ASM, fuel cost per gallon, labor unit costs, cargo/other revenue and nonfuel nonlabor costs. All assumptions are independent hypothetical analyst inputs—not actual Delta guidance.

## Sensitivity
Vary **passenger yield (±2%)** against **fuel price (±10%)**, holding other model inputs constant. Present the resulting FY2026 GAAP-like operating income as a **what-if**, not an estimated probability range.

## Controls
Across all FY2021–FY2025 years check that revenue components sum to reported total, individual expense categories sum to reported GAAP expenses, and the difference between revenue and expense equals reported operating income. Check FY2024→FY2025 bridge and the zero-change sensitivity center.

## Limitations
Pandemic-recovery FY2021, GAAP/non-GAAP differences, refinery economics, fuel costs and hedging, covarying capacity and pricing, actual labor contracts, and exclusion of full cash-flow/balance-sheet planning all limit forecast accuracy.
