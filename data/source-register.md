## Source verification notes
- FY2025 consolidated GAAP revenue: **$63,364m**; FY2024: **$61,643m**; FY2023: **$58,048m** (2025 10-K).
- FY2025 consolidated GAAP operating income: **$5,822m**; FY2024: **$5,995m**; FY2023: **$5,521m** (2025 10-K).
- FY2025 fuel and related taxes: **$9,819m**, on **4,269m gallons**; FY2024: **$10,566m**, on **4,114m gallons**. Published per-gallon amounts are **rounded**.
- FY2021 revenue: **$29,899m** (2023 10-K); FY2021 is heavily affected by COVID recovery and government payroll support.

## Required conventions
1. Record revenue, expenses and operating income as **reported GAAP**; do not mix them with adjusted operating results.
2. Preserve passenger, cargo and other revenue components; **Other** revenue is not exclusively passenger fees.
3. Keep government grant recognition (FY2021) and the FY2023 pilot agreement expense as separately identified operating line items.
4. Distinguish issuer-reported CASM-Ex (**non-GAAP**) from the student's GAAP residual unit-cost calculations.
5. Where published price/gal rounding prevents exact dollar reconciliation, derive the *implied* unit cost as reported expense / gallons and label it accordingly.
6. Source operational units explicitly: ASM (million available seat miles), PRASM (cents per ASM), and gallons (million).
7. Future scenario assumptions must be clearly labeled as illustrative and not represented as actual management budget, historical fact, or current company guidance.

**Provenance standard:** In the raw data tables, provide links to filings and the cited page/section for each source series. Never fabricate unreported figures.
