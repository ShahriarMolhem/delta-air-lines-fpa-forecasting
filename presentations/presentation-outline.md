**Presentation file:** `presentations/delta_fpa_executive_deck.pptx` (prepared; **pending GitHub binary upload**).

1. **Title:** Independent undergraduate FP&A study using Delta SEC data.
2. **Business questions:** Reported financial performance, operating-driver costs, scenarios.
3. **Historical context:** FY2021–2025 revenue and operating costs; pandemic effects.
4. **Operating margin:** FY2024 9.7% versus FY2025 9.2%.
5. **FY2024–2025 EBIT bridge:** passenger/cargo/other revenue, wages, fuel and other cost effects.
6. **Fuel price versus consumption:** implied unit expense calculation and expense decomposition.
7. **Hypothetical planning model:** ASM, PRASM, unit labor costs, gallons and unit fuel expense.
8. **Modeled operating results:** FY2026 $65.638bn revenue and $5.257bn EBIT, explicitly hypothetical.
9. **Sensitivity:** yield ±4% versus fuel cost ±20%; central cell $5.257bn.
10. **Conclusion and limitations:** student learning, disclosed simplifications, no real Delta budget.

**Design:** 16:9 PowerPoint (10 slides with speaker notes), suitable for a roughly 9–10-minute internship portfolio presentation.

**Presentation caution:** Do not say that scenario assumptions are current company forecasts, that price-volume algebra proves cause, or that the data include private management budgets.
